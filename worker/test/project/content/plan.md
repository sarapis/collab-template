# The plan

A short document of the kind this site is for: what the project will do, with
its open questions written beside the passages that raised them.

## What we are making {#making}

A shared place where the team writes what it knows, marks what it is guessing
at, and lets the people who know the answer write it in the margin.

:::question{slug=first-audience audience=team group=scope asked=r1}
Who is the first reader this site has to serve: the team itself, or people
outside it who will be pointed at a page?

**Why asked:** the answer decides whether reading stays private or opens up.
:::

## How we will know it worked {#measure}

Every figure on the site is computed by the build, never typed, so a page cannot
claim a number its content does not support.

:::question{slug=success-measure audience=reviewers group=scope asked=r1 margin=false see="#measure"}
What one observation, three months in, would tell us the site is being used
rather than merely kept?

**Why asked:** without it, "it works" is whatever the person asked decides.
:::
