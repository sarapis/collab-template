## One site for the documents and their questions {#one-site}

**Decision.** The documents and the questions they raise live on one site,
rather than the documents in one place and the questions in a tracker.

**Why.** A question beside the passage it is about can be answered by somebody
reading that passage, and the answer can be folded back into it.
